Delete your account

Scorched Knight, by Hayer Software Limited

In the game

  1. Open Scorched Knight (the Android app, or scorchedknight.com), signed in.
  2. Tap Settings (the cog) and find Cloud save.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It happens straight away. The hero saved on your device stays, so you can keep playing without an account.

Can't sign in?

Email support@thecaloriecard.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm it's you, delete everything and reply within one month (usually much sooner).

What's deleted

Your sign-in (email address and sign-in ID) and your cloud save. Nothing is kept, apart from Google Firebase's basic service logs, which are kept only for a short time.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
