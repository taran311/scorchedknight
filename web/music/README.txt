Scorched Knight - music

Drop five MP3 files in this folder with exactly these names:

  title.mp3   - the opening / hero select
  town.mp3    - the Soot & Kettle town square
  travel.mp3  - the world map and the trail
  battle.mp3  - ordinary fights
  boss.mp3    - lair bosses

Any missing file is simply skipped (silence there), so you can add them one at a time.
Tracks loop and crossfade when you move between places.

Free sources (check each track's licence and credit the composer if it asks):
  - incompetech.com (Kevin MacLeod, CC-BY)  e.g. medieval / fantasy categories
  - opengameart.org  (search "fantasy music", filter by CC0 or CC-BY)
  - freemusicarchive.org

Sound effects are made in code, so they need no files.
Volume, mute and the blood toggle are in Settings (the gear in town).
Sound starts after your first click or key press (browsers require it).
