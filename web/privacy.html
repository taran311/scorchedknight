The short version

You can play Scorched Knight without an account: your hero is saved on your device and nothing about you leaves it. If you choose to sign in, we keep a copy of your save in the cloud so you can carry your hero between your phone and the website. That's it. No adverts, no tracking, no selling your data.

What we collect

  • If you don't sign in: nothing. Your save stays on your device.
  • If you sign in (optional): your email address and a sign-in ID. With Google sign-in we also receive the name on your Google account; we don't use it for anything else and never post on your behalf. With email and password, your password is held by Google Firebase, never by us in readable form.
  • Your cloud save: your game progress (your hero, gear, gold and where you've been), with the time it was saved and a one-line summary (such as your hero's level) so the game can tell you which save is newer.
  • Buying the full game: Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details; the game only learns that the full game was bought on your Google account, and keeps that on your device.
  • Technical: Google Firebase keeps basic logs (such as time and error messages) to keep sign-in and the database running.

The game's lettering is downloaded from Google Fonts the first time you play, which means Google sees your device's internet address, as with any website.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your cloud save, so your hero goes wherever you play (performance of our contract with you).
  • To send essential emails, such as checking your email address and resetting a password, if you sign in with email.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems (our legitimate interests).

We don't sell your data, we don't show adverts, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we don't use your data to train AI models.

How long we keep it, and deleting your data

We keep your account and cloud save while you have an account. You can delete both at any time from inside the game: open Settings (the cog) → Cloud save → Delete account. Your sign-in and cloud save are deleted straight away; the hero saved on your device stays, so you can keep playing. More on the delete your account page.

Can't sign in? Email support@thecaloriecard.com from the email address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. Email us and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Storage on your device

The game stores your hero and settings (sound, music, your hero's name) on your device so it can carry on where you left off, and keeps you signed in if you chose to sign in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Scorched Knight is a fantasy adventure with cartoon combat, made for players aged 13 and over. Signing in is for players aged 13 and over. If we find out an account belongs to someone younger, we'll delete it and its cloud save.

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the game when it matters.
